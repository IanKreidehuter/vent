stay still. read slowly. move closer and it flinches.

I'm afraid of being abandoned after I finally let someone close.

I'm afraid of being chosen for a moment, then discarded when I stop being convenient, useful, entertaining, or easy to love.

I'm afraid that kindness is only the beginning of another manipulation.

I'm afraid of trusting someone, giving them my heart, and finding out that I was the only one taking it seriously.

I'm afraid of being misunderstood, because when someone misunderstands me, I feel like I have to explain myself over and over just to prove that my intentions were never what they thought.

I'm afraid of not knowing. I'm afraid of unanswered questions, changed behavior, silence, hidden things, and the empty space where an explanation should be. My mind wants to fill that space, because uncertainty feels unbearable.

I'm afraid of needing someone too much.

I'm afraid that if I love someone deeply, they will somehow gain the power to destroy my peace just by leaving.

I'm afraid of being nobody's first choice.

I'm afraid that people will eventually see something wrong with me and decide I'm not worth staying for.

I'm afraid of becoming useless. Of failing. Of not being as capable as everyone thinks I am.

I'm afraid of disappointing people who expect something from me.

I'm afraid of becoming so guarded that nobody can reach me anymore.

And somehow, I'm also afraid of the opposite: opening the door again and getting hurt by the same kind of story.

I'm afraid of losing things that feel like they're mine and realizing I never had control over whether they stayed.

I'm afraid of having to keep surviving without ever getting to rest.

I'm afraid that I'll spend years waiting for someone to choose me while my own life passes by.

I'm afraid that the little boy inside me will keep waiting for someone to come take care of him, and that I'll never be able to give him what he needed.

And underneath all of that, I think there's one fear that connects almost everything:

I'm afraid that I can love with my whole heart and still not be enough to make someone stay.

someday I'll get it
